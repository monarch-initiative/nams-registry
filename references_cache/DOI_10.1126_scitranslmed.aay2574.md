Vis, Joris van de Haar, Warner Prevoo, Petur Snaebjornsson, Daphne van der Velden, Michelle Klein, Myriam Chalabi, Henk Boot, Monique van Leerdam, Haiko J. Bloemendal, Laurens V. **Journal:** Science Translational Medicine (2019)
**DOI:** [10.1126/scitranslmed.aay2574](https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aay2574)

## Content

Tumor organoids from individual patients with metastatic colorectal cancer can help predict response to irinotecan with or without 5-fluorouracil.
